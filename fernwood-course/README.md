# Fernwood Health Clinic: Patient Privacy Rounds (fictional)

Inputs for the eLearning game skill. Fernwood and every person named here are fictional.

## sources/ (the facts)

- `privacy-policy-front-desk.md`: six rules, each settling one decision with one right answer and a key phrase.
- `common-mistakes.md`: what staff actually got wrong, grouped by rule. Use these as the wrong answers.
- `mock-audit-notes.md`: the business problem, audience constraints, and goal.

The six decisions:

1. A caller asks about a family member's information.
2. A caller says they are the patient.
3. Someone else's printed records are left in the printer.
4. A colleague discusses a patient where others can overhear.
5. A coworker's workstation is unlocked with a record open.
6. You want to open a record without a work reason.

## brand/ (the look and voice)

- `brand.md`: name, five colors, font, radius, voice summary, icon list, contrast notes.
- `voice.md`: the charge-nurse voice, with rules and examples.
- `logo.svg`: fern mark and wordmark.
- `icons/`: 16 SVGs, one per icon name in `brand.md`.
