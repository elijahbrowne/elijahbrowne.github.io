# Brand kit: Fernwood Health Clinic (fictional)

Name: Fernwood Health Clinic
Tagline: Care that stays between us.
Logo: logo.svg
Icons: icons/ (one SVG per name in the list below)

## Colours
accent: #1F6B5C      (the brand colour; fern green for buttons, progress, highlights)
ink: #17282B         (near-black with a green cast for text)
surface: #FFFFFF     (cards, case cards, the audit report)
bg: #F2F7F5          (page background; a pale clinic-wall mint)
accent-2: #F4B740    (warm amber for the "!" case markers, icons and small accents)

## Type
font: "Atkinson Hyperlegible", "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif
radius: 12px

## Voice
Calm and steady, like a good nurse on a busy day.
Say what to do, then say why, in one short sentence each.
Never blame. A wrong choice is a chance to practise, not a failure.
Plain words over policy words: "patient information", not "PHI", unless teaching the term.

Icon names: shield, lock, phone, printer, chat, computer, clipboard, folder, alert, check, cross, person, people, envelope, map, trophy
